DSBA MedTech Solutions

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Name: DSBA MedTech Solutions (Lima, Peru)

Reason: The name merges the initials of the founder (David Spencer Bellon Asencio - DSBA) with "MedTech" to form a brand identity. It represents an authorized distributor and technical support provider for specialized medical machinery based in Lima, serving hospitals and private clinics across Peru.

Site Purpose

The site serves as an interactive product catalog and quote request system for healthcare institutions in Lima and regional Peru. It showcases high-demand biomedical equipment including mechanical ventilators, suction pumps, HemoCue point-of-care analyzers, centrifuges, and patient monitors from recognized international brands. Medical staff can browse by category, review technical specifications, and request sales quotes or maintenance services online.

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: A clinic manager in Lima needs to request a fast quote for ICU mechanical ventilators and portable suction pumps for an urgent facility expansion.
  • Scenario 2: A laboratory supervisor in Peru is looking for HemoCue hemoglobin analyzers and clinical centrifuges with localized warranty and technical maintenance in Lima.

Color Schema

The color palette conveys clinical trust, high technology, and modern medical standards.

Primary (#005A9C) Headers, main branding, action buttons
Secondary (#00A896) Highlights, feature tags, interactive elements
Dark Neutral (#2B2D42) Main body text and footer background
Light Neutral (#F4F7F6) Page background and product card containers

Typography

Heading Font: Montserrat (Sans-serif) - Used for all titles, headers, and section banners to deliver a clean, technical, and professional aesthetic.

Body Font: Open Sans (Sans-serif) - Used for product specifications, descriptions, lists, and contact forms to ensure readability across mobile and desktop screens.
